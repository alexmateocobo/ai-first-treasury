    Introduction

    Welcome

    This survey assesses the readiness of Treasury Risk Management processes for agentic AI: autonomous AI systems that make independent decisions, coordinate with other agents, and adapt based on feedback.

    Survey structure

    The core of the survey covers four lifecycle steps of the Risk Management process, each assessed across five perspectives.

    Part ARespondent & Company9 questions
    Part BReadiness Assessment4 steps × 5 perspectives
    Part CProcess Debt4 ratings
    Part DAI Vision4 open questions

    Data privacy

    Your responses are processed by Louis Gentzsch and Alejandro Mateo Cobo (TUM School of Management, Chair of Controlling) exclusively for this academic project study. The survey is anonymous: no names, email addresses or company names are collected. Data is stored securely and will only be reported in aggregated form. No individual responses will be shared with third parties. Because responses are anonymous, individual submissions cannot be identified and therefore cannot be deleted after submission.

    Retention and deletion: Individual responses are retained only until the project study has been completed, and in any case no longer than 31 March 2027. After this date, all individual-level data is permanently deleted. Only aggregated results are kept for the final report.

    Part C

    Process Debt Assessment

    For each lifecycle step, rate how much your actual process deviates from the documented or intended process. Process debt reflects undocumented workarounds, informal practices, and gaps between how things should work and how they actually work.

    Scale

    Note that this scale runs in the opposite direction: a higher score means greater process debt.

    1Fully alignedActual process matches documentation; no workarounds.
    2Minor deviationsOccasional shortcuts that don't affect outcomes.
    3Moderate gapsSome steps rely on undocumented tribal knowledge; new employees need extensive mentoring.
    4Significant deviations"Shadow processes" (Excel workarounds, email-based approvals) run parallel to official systems.
    5ChaoticNo reliable documentation; process depends entirely on individual knowledge and ad hoc coordination.
